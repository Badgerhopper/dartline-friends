# DartLine Friends Edition: source and license notices

DartLine adapts the open-source [InstaDarts](https://github.com/Instadarts/instadarts), distributed under **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 only (AGPL-3.0-only)**. Camera recognition models, calibration, motion detection, real-time match servers, corrections, and much of the UI are original InstaDarts work.

DartLine modifications authored November 2026 include Cricket/Cut-Throat Cricket, Around the Clock, simplified friends-game labels, iPhone home-screen metadata, and a same-device scoring-camera dock with automatic pairing. The DartLine modifications are also AGPL-3.0-only. This is not endorsed by, sponsored by or affiliated with InstaDarts.
